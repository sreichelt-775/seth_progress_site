Relative humidity tests

Salt-suction testing and ongoing progress

The relative humidity testing is progressing as expected. K₂CO₃ now has good salt-suction data, and I will be moving to a new salt once I can get the lid off the current system. I can see how there were troubles in the past removing this after pressurization beyond 100°C.

The current ongoing tests at 100°C have not yet been checked for check weights. I am also unsure whether I should allow check weights because I would need to wait for the temperature to reduce before opening and releasing the pressure inside a boiling system, which could cause water to splash or harm the specimens or myself.

Because of that, I may wait until the known minimum time window from the other tests, which is about seven weeks. That falls in the first week of September, when I can pull these for final measurement prior to increasing to the next temperature step. I will touch base with you on this the next time we speak.

For now, I am continuing salt-suction data monitoring and monitoring the volumetric expansion of the 100% humidity specimens in the room-temperature distilled-water Tupperware.
